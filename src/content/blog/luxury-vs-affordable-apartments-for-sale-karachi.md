Karachi's real estate market offers two distinct paths for buyers: premium luxury residencies and budget-friendly affordable housing schemes. Both serve different needs, and understanding the trade-offs is essential to making the right choice.

### Luxury Apartments (e.g. City Executive Tower, Sun Residency)
Luxury apartments target buyers who prioritize premium lifestyle features, prime addresses, and high ROI.
- **Prime Locations:** PECHS, Main Shahrah-e-Faisal.
- **Premium Features:** Double-glazed glass window frames, high-speed elevators, luxury receptions, on-site saunas, gyms, and jacuzzis.
- **Higher Prices:** Starting prices around PKR 1.7 Crore+, reflecting land values and imported fitting materials.
- **ROI:** High rental yield from corporate executives and expatriates.

### Affordable Housing (e.g. Afroz Residency & Mobile Mall)
Affordable apartments focus on practicality, offering families clean, secure shelter without stretching their budgets to breaking point.
- **Developing Areas:** Korangi, Scheme 33, Malir.
- **Core Amenities:** 24/7 security, standard passenger elevators, clean municipal water, and dedicated parking.
- **Flexible Plans:** Starting prices around PKR 35 Lakh, with 3-5 year installment plans.
- **Objective:** Secure home ownership and monthly savings.
