Receiving possession of a new apartment in Karachi is an important milestone, but the handover should not be treated as a simple formality. Before you sign the final paperwork and accept the keys, you must perform a thorough inspection of the property to ensure everything matches your agreement.

Here is a comprehensive checklist designed for apartment buyers in Karachi:

### 1. Structural Checks and Finishes
- **Walls and Ceilings:** Check for dampness (seepage), which is a common issue in Karachi apartments. Inspect for cracks in plaster and uneven paint finishes.
- **Tiles and Flooring:** Tap tiles gently to ensure there are no hollow sounds underneath, indicating poor cement bonding. Check for chipped corners and uniform grouting.

### 2. Plumbing and Sanitary
- **Fittings and Taps:** Turn on all taps to check water pressure and ensure there are no leaks. Inspect under sinks and washbasins.
- **Drainage:** Pour water in the bathroom, kitchen, and balcony drains to verify they flow smoothly and do not clog.

### 3. Electrical Systems
- **Switchboards and Sockets:** Use a simple tester to verify that all electrical points have active power.
- **Wiring Safety:** Ensure the main circuit breaker box is clearly labeled and wires are completely insulated.

### 4. Doors and Windows
- **Functionality:** Open and close all doors and windows to make sure they swing or slide smoothly without grating.
- **Locks and Latches:** Verify that all locking mechanisms work securely.

### 5. Legal and Approvals
- **SBCA Approvals:** Ensure the developer has obtained the final completion certificate from the Sindh Building Control Authority (SBCA) for the building.
